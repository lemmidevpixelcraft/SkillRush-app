SkillRush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SkillRush is designed to work without a developer account or a developer-operated analytics service.

Data stored on your device

SkillRush stores gameplay progress, settings, skill ratings, achievements and recent run history locally on your device using Apple platform storage.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, SkillRush uses Apple GameKit to submit leaderboard scores and completed achievement identifiers. Game Center identity and Game Center records are handled by Apple and are subject to Apple’s privacy practices.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple using StoreKit. SkillRush reads purchase entitlements to determine whether Pro features are unlocked. SkillRush does not receive your payment card details.

Advertising and analytics

The current release contains no third-party advertising SDK and no third-party analytics SDK. SkillRush does not upload your run history to a developer-operated analytics server.

Retention and deletion

Local SkillRush data remains on the device until the app or its data is removed. Deleting the app removes local gameplay data from that device. Game Center data and App Store purchase records are managed by Apple and may remain associated with your Apple Account.

Changes

This policy may be updated when SkillRush features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details published on the SkillRush Support page.
