SkillRush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SkillRush is designed to work without a developer account or a developer-operated analytics service. The free iOS edition is supported by advertising. SkillRush Pro removes SkillRush advertising.

Data stored on your device

SkillRush stores gameplay progress, settings, skill ratings, achievements, advertising cadence information and recent run history locally on your device using Apple platform storage. SkillRush does not upload your local run history to a developer-operated analytics server.

Advertising — free iOS edition

The free iOS edition uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display a compact Home-screen banner and occasional interstitial ads at natural breaks after completed Quick Runs. Ads are not displayed during active challenges. SkillRush Pro disables SkillRush ad requests and ad placements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, diagnostics and performance, which can include IP address (for approximate location), device or app identifiers, advertising data, ad impressions and interactions, performance information and crash/diagnostic information. The exact handling can depend on region, privacy choices and Google's services.

Where required, SkillRush uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising privacy choices before requesting ads. If Google requires a persistent privacy-options entry point, SkillRush exposes it in Settings → Advertising.

SkillRush 1.0 does not itself request App Tracking Transparency permission for IDFA access. This may change in a future release only if the app's advertising setup changes, in which case this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users can access an ad-reporting link from SkillRush Settings → Advertising. Google also provides ad information and reporting controls on eligible ads.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, SkillRush uses Apple GameKit to submit leaderboard scores and completed achievement identifiers. Game Center identity and Game Center records are handled by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy practices.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple using StoreKit. SkillRush reads purchase entitlements to determine whether Pro features and the ad-free experience are unlocked. SkillRush does not receive your payment card details.

Developer analytics

SkillRush does not use a separate developer-operated third-party analytics product and does not create a developer account profile for players. Advertising services may nevertheless process ad-related usage, measurement and diagnostic information as described above.

Retention and deletion

Local SkillRush data remains on the device until the app or its data is removed. Deleting the app removes local gameplay data from that device. Game Center data and App Store purchase records are managed by Apple and may remain associated with your Apple Account. Advertising data handled by Google is subject to Google's applicable policies and privacy choices.

Changes

This policy may be updated when SkillRush features, advertising configuration or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details published on the SkillRush Support page.
