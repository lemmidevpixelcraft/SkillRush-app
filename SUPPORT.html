Purchase help

Use “Restore purchases” inside SkillRush if a previously purchased Pro unlock is not recognized. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account used for the transaction. SkillRush Pro also removes SkillRush advertising.

Advertising

The free iOS edition can display Google AdMob ads. Advertising privacy choices, when required, are available in SkillRush Settings → Advertising. The same section includes a link for reporting an inappropriate or age-inappropriate ad.

Google: report an ad

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If leaderboards do not load, verify that Game Center is enabled and signed in on the device.
